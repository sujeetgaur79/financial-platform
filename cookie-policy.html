Tracking & Technology Disclosures Updated: November 2026

Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies and tracking technologies are, how WealthPulse utilizes them, and how you can exercise full control over your cookie preferences.

1 What Are Cookies?

A cookie is a small text file that a website places on your computer, tablet, or smartphone when you visit. Cookies help the website remember your actions and preferences (such as selected currency, saved bookmarks, or interface state) over a period of time, so you don't have to keep re-entering them whenever you come back to the site.

2 Categories of Cookies Used on WealthPulse

Essential & Functional Storage

Always Active

These technologies are strictly necessary for the site to function properly. WealthPulse uses browser LocalStorage to remember your preferred currency symbol ($, €, £, ₹), your saved article bookmarks, and your interactive calculator parameters so you don't lose progress during a session. These contain zero personally identifiable data.

Analytics & Performance Cookies

Optional

These cookies collect aggregated, anonymous information about how visitors navigate the platform—such as which masterclasses are read most frequently and average reading times. This data enables our research desk to refine content depth.

Google AdSense & Advertising Cookies

Third-Party Managed

Google AdSense and its certified advertising vendors may set cookies (including DoubleClick DART cookies) to serve relevant advertisements to users based on prior visits to WealthPulse and other sites across the web. You have the right to decline or opt out of personalized ad targeting at any time.

3 How to Manage or Opt Out of Advertising Cookies

You can control and disable personalized advertising cookies through official industry opt-out registries:

  • Google Ads Settings: You can manage how Google personalizes your ads or disable ad personalization entirely at https://adssettings.google.com/.
  • Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA): Opt out of participating ad networks at https://optout.aboutads.info/.
  • Network Advertising Initiative (NAI): Opt out at https://optout.networkadvertising.org/.

4 Managing Cookies in Your Web Browser

You can configure your browser to reject all cookies, alert you when a cookie is placed, or delete existing cookies. Follow the links below for browser-specific instructions:

Google Chrome → Mozilla Firefox → Apple Safari → Microsoft Edge →

Please note: If you block all cookies, certain features (like saving your calculator preferences or offline reading bookmarks) will reset each time you refresh the page.
