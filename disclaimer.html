2 Calculator Assumptions & Mathematical Modeling

WealthPulse provides interactive calculators to help users understand mathematical relationships (e.g., compound interest, debt interest amortization, 50/30/20 proportions). These calculators operate on user-provided inputs and constant compounding assumptions:

  • Compound Growth Calculator: Assumes a constant, unvarying annual rate of return (e.g., 7% or 8%). In actual financial markets, returns fluctuate substantially year-to-year, and investors face sequence-of-returns risk and bear markets.
  • Debt Snowball & Avalanche: Assumes fixed interest rates, uniform minimum monthly payments, and zero subsequent borrowing or penalty fees.
  • 50/30/20 Budgeting: Represents a baseline heuristic; high-cost-of-living metropolitan areas often require tailored proportions (e.g., 60/20/20 or 70/20/10).
