Affiliate & Advertising Disclosure

In compliance with Federal Trade Commission (FTC) guidelines (16 CFR Part 255), this disclosure outlines how WealthPulse operates as a 100% free financial educational resource while maintaining total transparency regarding our revenue sources.

Our Core Monetization Philosophy

All guides, financial simulators, interactive calculators, and AI assistance on WealthPulse are provided completely free of charge to readers. We do not sell paywalled subscriptions or lock calculators behind fees. To support hosting, ongoing engineering development, and our research desk, we rely on non-intrusive display advertisements (Google AdSense) and selected, transparent affiliate partnerships.

1 Display Advertising (Google AdSense)

WealthPulse displays advertisements managed by Google AdSense across designated areas of our articles and home hubs.

  • Ad slots are always clearly bounded and labeled with the word "ADVERTISEMENT".
  • Advertisements are delivered algorithmically by Google based on user interests or page context.
  • Advertisers do not select, inspect, or approve any editorial articles or calculator formulas prior to publication.

2 Affiliate Relationships & Referral Links

Some links on our platform may be affiliate links. This means that if you click on an external link to an FDIC-insured High-Yield Savings Account (HYSA), a budgeting utility, or a credit monitoring service and subsequently open an account or purchase a tool, WealthPulse may receive a small referral commission at zero additional cost to you.

In many cases, our affiliate links provide you with discounted rates or enhanced introductory offers that are not publicly available otherwise.

3 Editorial Independence Guarantee

Our editorial opinions, computational math, and debt payoff evaluations are strictly our own. We uphold the following commitments:

  • No Favorable Coverage for Compensation: A financial institution cannot pay us to receive a favorable review, inflate its rating, or suppress competitive alternatives.
  • Formula Integrity: Our debt payoff calculators calculate mathematical interest totals objectively. Affiliate partnerships have zero technical capability to influence the mathematical output of our tools.
  • Strict Anti-Predatory Rule: We adamantly reject partnerships with payday lenders, high-APR title loan operators, and get-rich-quick speculative trading schemes.

4 Questions Regarding Partnerships

If you have questions regarding any specific link, sponsored placement, or advertising partner on WealthPulse, please contact our transparency team at transparency@wealthpulse.app.
